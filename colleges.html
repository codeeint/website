Do we need to buy any hardware ourselves, or does Codeeint supply everything?

Codeeint supplies all hardware — NVIDIA Jetson kits, robotics kits, cables, SD cards, pre-installed JetPack and everything required for the lab. The institution provides the physical space, tables, projector and a timetable slot. You do not need to purchase or configure anything separately.

Do our faculty need to know NVIDIA / AI to run this lab?

No prior knowledge required. Codeeint's expert trainer works alongside your faculty from Day One — training them hands-on through real sessions with students. By Month 3, your staff are certified to run sessions independently. Codeeint provides all session content, lab manuals, assessment documents and support materials so your faculty never have to create anything from scratch. The FDP (Faculty Development Programme) formalises this into an NVIDIA DLI Instructor certification pathway for interested staff.

How does the NVIDIA DLI certification process work for students?

NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute (DLI) certifications are earned through NVIDIA's own online assessment platform. Codeeint prepares students through hands-on project work throughout the program. When students are ready, Codeeint facilitates their access to the DLI assessment portal and guides them through the certification process. Students receive NVIDIA-issued digital certificates directly from NVIDIA.

We already have an Atal Tinkering Lab or basic AI lab. Can Codeeint work alongside it?

Yes — Codeeint integrates, not replaces. Many institutions run existing Arduino or basic AI setups alongside Codeeint's NVIDIA lab. We map the curriculum to begin where your existing program ends, ensuring no duplication. Codeeint specifically fills the gap between "basic tinkering" and "industry-ready NVIDIA AI engineering."

How does the 12-month support SLA work in practice?

The SLA covers: dedicated technical helpdesk (hardware or software issues), trainer revisit for any session or topic that needs remediation, curriculum update access as NVIDIA releases new frameworks, half-year and end-year progress reviews with lab coordinator, and hardware replacement coordination. This is active support — not a warranty card.

Can Codeeint labs be documented for NAAC / NBA accreditation?

Yes. Every Codeeint installation includes a NAAC/NBA-ready documentation package — lab specifications, equipment list, curriculum overview, session logs, student outcome records and certification data. Codeeint also provides an official lab description document for the institution's accreditation submission. Many institutions have used Codeeint's AI lab as a key criterion 5 or 6 differentiator.

What is the minimum batch size / room requirement?

Minimum batch size is 15 students. Codeeint works with batches of 15–60 students per lab room. For larger cohorts, we set up multiple batches with separate session slots. Room requirement is a standard classroom or lab space — no special renovation or dedicated infrastructure needed. One projector or smartboard, standard power sockets and a stable internet connection are all you need to provide.
