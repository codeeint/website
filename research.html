Researcher FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to the technical and logistical questions we get from R&D partners.

Do you take equity in the startups you help?

No. Codeeint is a technical infrastructure and R&D partner. We work on a service/consultancy basis, ensuring you retain 100% of your IP and equity.

Can you help with custom hardware integration for my specific AI application?

Absolutely. We specialize in selecting and integrating non-standard sensors, custom PCBs, and industrial motor controllers. Our team ensures that your specific hardware stack is optimized for your AI models and ready for rugged industrial environments.

Do you provide support for migrating research code to an edge-optimized production environment?

Yes. We bridge the gap between "it works on my machine" and "it works in production." We help you containerize your research, optimize it using NVIDIA TensorRT, and deploy it onto edge devices with a robust OTA update pipeline.
