Questions & Answers

Questions

Everything a principal, HoD or purchase committee wants to know — answered clearly and honestly.

Ask Us Directly →

Do we need to purchase hardware separately, or does Codeeint supply everything?

Codeeint supplies everything — BBC Micro:bit kits, sensors, components, cables, and all accessories, fully assembled and ready to use. The school does not need to purchase, source or configure any hardware separately. You provide the room, tables, a projector and a timetable slot. Everything else comes from Codeeint.

Our teachers don't know robotics or AI. Can they still run this program?

Yes — that is exactly what Codeeint is built for. Our expert trainer comes to your school and works alongside your teachers from Day One, training them hands-on through real student sessions. By Month 2–3, your staff are certified to run every session independently. Codeeint provides the full session plan, teacher guide and all materials. Your teachers don't need to prepare anything from scratch.

Which grades does the program cover and how is it structured?

The Codeeint program covers Grade 4 to Grade 12 across three kit tiers. Foundation (Grade 4–6) uses BBC Micro:bit with MakeCode and basic Python. Advanced (Grade 7–9) adds AI Vision Camera and IoT Cloud. Expert (Grade 10–12) introduces computer vision, OpenCV, AI model training and YOLO. Each grade has 60 hours of content per year — 30 theory and 30 practical — mapped to CBSE Science and Maths chapters and aligned with NEP 2020.

What documents and materials does Codeeint provide?

Codeeint provides a printed lab manual for every student, a teacher guide with full session delivery plan, student assessment sheets, project evaluation forms, student completion certificates and all NAAC/board accreditation-ready documentation. Your school does not need to create any content. Everything is handed over on Day One and updated each academic year.

We already have an Atal Tinkering Lab. Do we still need Codeeint?

Many schools run both. ATL is great for open tinkering and government compliance — but it was never designed to produce structured AI engineers. ATL leaves teaching to the school teacher after 8 days of onboarding, with no fixed timetable and no ongoing trainer support. Codeeint picks up from where ATL stops — structured grade-wise AI curriculum, trained staff and all documentation. We integrate with ATL, not replace it.

How many students can participate per batch?

Each batch can have 20 to 40 students, with one kit per student pair. For schools with larger student strength, Codeeint sets up multiple batches with separate timetable slots. The minimum batch size is 15 students. There is no maximum — larger schools can run 3 to 4 batches across different grades simultaneously.

Is this program aligned with CBSE, ICSE and NEP 2020?

Yes. Codeeint's curriculum is fully aligned with NEP 2020's emphasis on experiential, competency-based learning. Each grade's project topics are cross-linked to Science and Maths chapters from CBSE, ICSE, IB and major State Board syllabi. Schools can use Codeeint's lab activity records directly in their curriculum documentation and NAAC submissions.

What happens if equipment breaks or a teacher gets stuck?

Codeeint's 12-month active SLA covers hardware issues, curriculum questions and staff support. If a kit malfunctions, Codeeint coordinates replacement. If a teacher is stuck on a topic, a refresher session is scheduled. If any content needs updating, Codeeint provides the update. You contact Codeeint once — we resolve it. The school never has to figure out hardware or content issues alone.

What certificate do students receive at the end?

Students receive a Codeeint grade-completion certificate at the end of each academic year. The certificate mentions the grade completed, skills learned, projects built and the school name. For Grade 10–12 students, the certificate includes AI and computer vision skills that carry weight in engineering entrance portfolios. Codeeint also provides a student project record document listing all 10+ projects completed.

What does the school need to provide — and what does Codeeint provide?

The school provides four things: a room (existing classroom works), tables (one per student pair), a projector or smartboard, and a timetable slot (one session per batch per week). Codeeint provides everything else — all hardware, curriculum, teacher training, printed student manuals, assessment forms, certificates, NAAC documentation and 12-month support. Your school runs the program. Codeeint makes sure you can.
